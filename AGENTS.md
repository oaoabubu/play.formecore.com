# AGENTS.md — topic content operations

1. Read `memory/` before working on topics.
2. For topic generation or scoring, use
   `$formecore-topic-scorer` and follow its referenced rubric and SoT schema.
3. Read the existing `topics/library/` and `topics/selection/queue.md` before
   proposing anything. One search intent should normally have one canonical
   topic.
4. Do not invent search volume, product facts, competitor features, founder
   stories, user feedback, or performance data. Label demand evidence as
   `measured`, `observed`, `inferred`, or `unverified`.
5. Daily discovery may create new files under `topics/library/` and `runs/`.
   It must not overwrite an existing topic SoT.
6. Public content is never published by topic discovery. Blog and social
   deliverables remain `planned` until a separate production and approval flow
   runs.
7. Prefer `skipped` or `needs-human` over filling the library with weak or
   duplicate topics.
