# Product source of truth — FORMECORE × Berry

## Positioning

FORMECORE × Berry is a free browser companion game by FORMECORE in Traditional Chinese and
English. Berry is the quiet virtual cat inside the game and accompanies focus sessions and
movement sessions without punishing missed days.

## Verified product capabilities

- Focus timer presets include 25/5, 50/10, and 15/3, with custom durations.
- Movement can be recorded by elapsed time, countdown segments, or manual
  repetitions and sets.
- Completing activities can award companion points, treasures, and memories.
- After a completed focus or movement session, Berry can return with a small gift.
- Players can decorate the room with items such as wallpaper, curtains, clocks,
  rugs, banners, and cat furniture.
- Missing a day, ending early, or missing a daily goal does not remove existing
  progress or make the pet sick.
- No account is required. The current version stores progress in the user's
  browser and supports manual backup and restore.
- Browser storage is tied to the current origin and does not automatically sync
  between devices or browsers.
- Mobile background throttling and lock-screen behaviour can delay sound; the
  product must not promise a perfectly timed lock-screen alert.

## Priority audiences

1. People who want a gentle focus timer rather than a strict productivity app.
2. Students and home workers who enjoy a quiet virtual-pet companion.
3. People who want one lightweight browser tool for focus and movement logs.
4. Users who prefer trying a tool without creating an account.

## Claims boundary

- Do not claim to treat ADHD, anxiety, loneliness, or another condition.
- Do not guarantee better focus, productivity, habit formation, fitness,
  weight loss, or health outcomes.
- Do not describe browser-local data as cloud sync.
- Do not hide material product limitations in comparisons or rankings.
