# Topic types for play.formecore.com

Use these as a balanced candidate map, not a requirement to produce every category. Prefer topics that match verified product facts and a distinct user intent.

## 1. Direct category discovery

Users already know the type of tool they want.

- 貓咪番茄鐘
- 虛擬寵物專注計時器
- 療癒系專注遊戲
- 線上運動計時器
- 專注與運動二合一工具

## 2. Rankings and roundups

Users are building a shortlist. Define inclusion criteria, test products, disclose ownership, and give different winners by use case.

- 最佳虛擬寵物專注 App
- 免費免註冊番茄鐘推薦
- 瀏覽器養貓遊戲盤點
- Cozy Productivity 工具推薦

Do not rank Berry first by default. Rank it only where the method supports the result.

## 3. Comparisons and alternatives

Users are choosing between approaches or products.

- 番茄鐘與普通計時器怎麼選
- 按時間與按次數記錄運動的差別
- 本機儲存與雲端同步的差別
- 單人寵物陪伴與多人共同專注遊戲比較

## 4. Explanations and questions

Users want a concise definition or answer.

- 貓咪番茄鐘是什麼
- 虛擬寵物如何陪伴專注
- 為什麼換瀏覽器後遊戲進度不見了
- 為什麼 Berry 不懲罰斷簽

## 5. How-to and settings

Users want to complete a task.

- 如何開始一次 15 分鐘專注
- 25/5、50/10、15/3 怎麼選
- 如何設定居家運動區段和休息時間
- 如何備份與恢復 Berry 資料

## 6. Problems and situations

Users describe their current difficulty rather than a product category.

- 打卡中斷後如何重新開始
- 不喜歡高壓效率工具，可以用什麼
- 在家工作如何安排專注與活動休息
- 不想下載或註冊帳號的專注工具

## 7. Trust, privacy, and limitations

Users are deciding whether the product is safe or suitable.

- Berry 的資料儲存在哪裡
- 手機鎖屏後瀏覽器計時是否可靠
- Berry 適合誰、不適合誰
- 免費瀏覽器遊戲會保存哪些資料

## 8. Product stories and first-party evidence

These differentiate the site when supported by real records.

- 為什麼我們把專注和運動放在同一隻貓裡
- 為什麼 Berry 在專注期間保持安靜
- 我們如何測試中斷、重整與重複結算
- 哪些計時設定最常被使用

Never fabricate founder stories, user feedback, usage data, or test outcomes.

## Candidate mix

For a broad generation request, aim for a useful mix rather than equal quotas:

- direct category and high-intent choice topics;
- rankings, roundups, and comparisons;
- questions and how-to topics;
- product trust and first-party differentiation.

Avoid producing many titles that differ only by year, number, or synonym. One search intent should normally have one canonical page.
