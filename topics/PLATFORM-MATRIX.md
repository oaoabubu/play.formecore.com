## Platform-native format rules

| Platform | Use for these topics | Native format |
|---|---|---|
| Blog | Search intent, durable comparison, help, and citations | Full HTML article with tables, FAQ, evidence, and FORMECORE × Berry CTA |
| Xiaohongshu | Saveable choices, emotional tension, and visible steps | Cover plus 6–8 cards and native caption; avoid relying on an outbound body link |
| WeChat Official Account | Long-form education and shareable Chinese guides | Native article with opening conclusion, screenshots, section anchors, and QR/link CTA after review |
| Medium | Durable English explanation for international discovery | English adaptation, not direct translation; state FORMECORE ownership and link the canonical source |
| TikTok | Fast visual proof, comparison, and before/after | 30–60 second result-first demo with on-screen labels and one CTA |
| Douyin | Chinese-native scenario, demonstration, and practical checklist | 30–60 second spoken Chinese video with fast visual proof and native caption |
| Threads | Judgment, comparison, and discussion | 5–8 native posts; each post stands alone and the product disclosure remains visible |
| Instagram | Saveable visual understanding | 6–8 card carousel or short Reel; one judgment per card |
| Facebook | Community trust and practical discussion | Group-safe answer, checklist, story, or question; no link dropping without context |
| YouTube Shorts | Search-adjacent visual explanation | 45–60 second hook, demonstration, decision rule, and CTA |

## Status vocabulary

- `planned`: approved channel opportunity, no asset yet.
- `draft`: native asset exists and awaits review.
- `review`: factual and editorial review in progress.
- `approved`: ready for a human to publish.
- `published`: live on the named platform.
- `not-recommended`: weak fit; produce only with a specific campaign reason.
